Sleep Well Sleep Tracker Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 25, 2026

1. Overview

Sleep Well Sleep Tracker helps users record sleep duration, review sleep trends, play white noise, and identify nighttime sound events such as ambient noise and suspected snoring. We design the app with local-first privacy in mind.

2. Information We Collect

The app does not require an account. Sleep records, sound events, snore samples, app settings, and white noise preferences are stored on your device by default.

  • Microphone access is used only after you actively start sleep monitoring.
  • Nighttime audio may be processed on device to detect noise and suspected snoring events.
  • Apple Health access is optional and used only after your authorization.

3. How We Use Information

We use sleep and sound information to generate sleep reports, show trends, count nighttime events, tune snore recognition, and restore your app settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not intentionally upload your sleep recordings or snore samples to our servers. If future features require cloud sync or online feedback submission, this policy will be updated before release.

5. Apple Health

If you authorize Apple Health integration, the app may read or write sleep-related health data according to the permissions you grant in iOS. You can change these permissions in the Health app or iOS Settings.

6. Storage and Security

Sleep data and audio samples are stored locally on your device. You can manage or delete local records in the app where available. Please keep your device and Apple account secure.

7. Children’s Privacy

Sleep Well Sleep Tracker is not intended for children and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when features or data handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
