WenJian Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

1. Overview

WenJian is a macOS Markdown note-taking and writing app. We designed it to work with local files first and to avoid unnecessary account systems or tracking.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

WenJian reads and writes Markdown files in the folder you choose. Your notes, titles, folders, and related Markdown content are stored locally or in the storage provider you select, such as iCloud Drive or another synced folder.

3. Cloud Sync

WenJian does not provide its own cloud account or server sync. If you place your notes in iCloud Drive, Nutstore, Dropbox, or another sync folder, synchronization is handled by that provider under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Data Collection

WenJian does not collect your note content, does not sell personal data, and does not track users across apps or websites for advertising.

5. Permissions

The app may request file or folder access so you can open and save Markdown files. This permission is used only to provide the editing and file management features you request.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete notes from the folders you manage. Removing the app from your Mac does not automatically delete Markdown files stored outside the app container.
