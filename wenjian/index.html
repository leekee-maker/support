WenJian for macOS / 文涧 macOS 版

本地优先的 Markdown 笔记与写作工具

WenJian helps you write, organize, and preview Markdown notes with a local-first workflow. It does not require an account and keeps your content in folders you choose.

文涧适合记录灵感、整理文档、编写技术笔记和准备演示内容。内容优先保存在你选择的本地文件夹中，不要求登录账号。
